# Informed Consent Agreement

## Study Title
Relative Roughness Survey - Surface Texture Assessment Study

## Purpose of the Research
This research aims to collect human perception data on the relative roughness of surfaces in images. Your participation will help improve our understanding of how people perceive and rate surface texture properties.

## What Participation Involves
- You will be shown images of various surfaces
- You will rate the relative roughness of each surface on a scale of 1-5
- Each rating session takes approximately 10-15 minutes
- You may participate in multiple sessions

## Confidentiality and Data Protection
- Your survey responses will be anonymized
- Data will be stored securely and used only for research purposes
- Your personal information will not be shared with third parties
- Results may be published in academic contexts without identifying you

## Voluntary Nature of Participation
- Your participation is entirely voluntary
- You may withdraw at any time without penalty
- There are no negative consequences for choosing not to participate or withdraw

## Risks and Benefits
- There are minimal risks associated with this study
- No direct benefits are provided for participation
- Your contribution will advance our understanding of human texture perception
