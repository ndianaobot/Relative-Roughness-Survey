Relative Roughness Survey

This is a survey to collect data on the relative roughness of the surface of the images. Rank each of the images from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest relative roughness.

Please ensure that each rating is different. If you have any questions, please contact Ndiana Obot at ndianaobot8@gmail.com
