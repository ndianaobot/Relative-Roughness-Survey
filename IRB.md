You are invited to participate in a research study. Please read this form carefully before agreeing to participate.

The purpose of this research is to study how people perceive surface roughness from images. Your responses will help us develop improved texture feedback systems for prosthetic devices, specifically the PALM (Prosthetic Adaptive Limb Module), which aims to provide sensory feedback to users of upper-limb prostheses.

If you agree to participate, you will:

• View 5 images showing different surface textures

• Rank these images 1-5 from smoothest (1) to roughest based on your perception

This survey will take approximately 5 minutes to complete.

**Your participation is completely voluntary.** You may choose not to participate or may withdraw from the study at any time without any penalty or consequence.

**Risks:** No risks are anticipated beyond those associated with typical computer use, such as minor eye strain or fatigue from viewing images on a screen. The survey is brief and does not involve sensitive topics.

**Compensation and Benefits:** You will not receive any compensation or incentives for participating in this study. You will not receive any direct benefits from participating in this study. However, your contribution will help advance research in prosthetic technology and may ultimately improve the quality of life for individuals with upper-limb differences.
